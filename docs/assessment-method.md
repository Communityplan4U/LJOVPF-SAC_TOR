# Assessment method

Only terms and commitments expressly in the written Terms of Reference are scored. The 12 consolidated criteria fall under five governance areas, weighted by participant position 30/25/20/15/10. Each component in an area receives an equal part of the area weight. Judgments compare the comparator TOR with LJOVPF: +1 means stronger written provisions in the comparator; 0 means comparable; -1 means stronger in LJOVPF. Missing evidence is **not** a zero. Weston in Gear remains unrated pending verification of its own written TOR. Source excerpts, section references and approval are mandatory for publication.

The author's approved default scores will be generated from the approved judgment matrix, not provisional or historical ratings. Participant reordering changes weights, not evidence.
