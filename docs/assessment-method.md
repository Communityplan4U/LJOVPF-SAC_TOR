# SAC Terms of Reference assessment method — approved November 22, 2026

The tool uses the **approved 12-component documentary comparison** from `LJOVPF_SAC_TOR_Comparison_Approved_Weighted_Key_Findings_Updated.xlsx`, with **120 approved judgments** across ten comparator advisory bodies. Weston in Gear is included using its supplied four-page written TOR, stored under `public/sources/weston-in-gear-lac-tor.pdf`.

Only provisions **expressly written in each Terms of Reference** are scored. These judgments do not evaluate later meeting practices, legal compliance, whether staff actually implemented the rules, or residents' experience of the committee.

## Fixed documentary judgments

- **+1:** comparator TOR is stronger than LJOVPF on the criterion.
- **0:** comparable written provisions, without a material overall advantage.
- **−1:** LJOVPF TOR is stronger.

Every judgment includes a reference-TOR provision summary, comparator-TOR provision summary, source section(s), assessment rationale and the originating approved workbook row. The summaries are not verbatim quotes. Missing or unapproved evidence must **never** be treated as zero.

## Default ranking and weighting

| Position | Area | Share |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | G1 — Community decision-making and influence | 30% |
| 2 | G2 — Timing and meaningful participation | 25% |
| 3 | G3 — Representation, equity and support | 20% |
| 4 | G5 — Transparency and public accountability | 15% |
| 5 | G4 — Committee governance and responsibilities | 10% |

The within-area weight is distributed **equally** among its criteria. The published net score for each comparator is `12 × sum(area weight × mean signed rating in the area)`. The original LJOVPF TOR is the zero-score reference; larger positive scores mean stronger comparator TORs. Ties share the same rank.

When a participant changes the order of the five areas, the **weights assigned to positions change**, but the documentary judgments and criteria remain fixed. Background and origin are addressed under criterion `G5-02` rather than as a separate fixed-score term.

The approved scores and ranks are documented in [the results summary](approved-results.md). The test suite checks all eleven displayed scores and ranks against the approved workbook, checks all 120 judgments and their evidence fields, verifies the Weston PDF hash, and rejects a partial published assessment.
